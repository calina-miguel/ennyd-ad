Four ways to work with local media

From a folder to the output you need.

Each workflow starts with an existing folder and ends with a useful result.

01 · Browse and inspect

See your media clearly.

Review images, video, audio, and supported documents in Table, Grid, or Gallery. Filter, sort, inspect metadata, zoom, rotate, step through video, and keep your selection as you change views.

02 · Transcribe and search speech

Search the words inside your media.

Transcribe eligible audio and video on your Mac. Search exact phrases, use Boolean grammar, separate Exact from Related results, and open a match at its source timestamp.

03 · Search by what you see

Follow a visual idea through the open folder.

Use a text description, an image reference, or the representative frame of a playing video. Ennyd ranks eligible images and video thumbnails without moving the files.

Visual Search ranks media items. It does not identify people or objects, analyze every video frame, or pinpoint an internal video timestamp.

04 · Moments and outputs

Turn a found Moment into useful output.

Open a transcript result at its source time, play selected video and audio ranges in order, export clips, write transcript sidecars, or review compatible sources before merging them.
